# Duo Arcade Support

For help with Duo Arcade on iPhone or the web, [open a support request](https://github.com/yanym/duo-arcade/issues/new).

Include the game name, app version, device and operating system, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help, but remove any personal information first. Do not post passwords, Apple account details, private room credentials, or other sensitive information in public issues.

## Connection problems

- Check that both players have an internet connection.
- Use **Reconnect** if the room offers it. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same move.
- If an invitation has expired, create a new room and share its new code.
- Keep the web page or app up to date before starting a new session.

## Playing with a friend

Create a friend room, share the six-character code, and wait for the other player to join. Both players must confirm they are ready. A friend can join from iPhone or the [web app](https://duo-arcade.yimgyan.workers.dev).

## Playing alone

Choose **Play with AI**, select a game, and confirm readiness. An internet connection is still required.

Language, sound, haptics, contrast, and motion preferences are available in Settings. Game-specific instructions can be reopened with **Help**.
